Quick check — can I use this tool?

1
Is the paper open access? Yes → You can use the tool freely.
2
Are you logged in via your institution and the paper is covered by your institutional subscription? Yes → You likely have access rights. Check your institution's policy on automated tools before proceeding.
3
Do you have no access to the paper? Stop → Do not use this tool. It is not designed to bypass paywalls or access content you are not entitled to.

Glossary

DOI
Digital Object Identifier. A unique persistent identifier for a document (e.g. 10.1613/jair.49).
Open access
A paper freely available to anyone online, typically under a Creative Commons license (CC-BY, CC-0).
Institutional access
Access granted through a subscription paid by your university or research institute.
Paywall
A barrier restricting access to content unless payment or authentication is provided.
Terms of Service (ToS)
Contractual rules set by a publisher governing how their platform can be used.
TDM
Text and Data Mining. The automated analysis of large amounts of digital text. Subject to specific legal exemptions in some jurisdictions.
