# Knowledge Base — Legal Guide for Users

> This document helps you quickly assess whether and how you can use the DOI downloader tool legally and ethically in your situation.

---

## Can I use this tool?

Use the questions below to assess your situation:

1. **Is the paper open access?**
   If yes → you can use the tool freely.

2. **Are you logged in to the publisher's platform via your institution?**
   If yes → you likely have access rights. Check your institution's policy on automated tools before proceeding.

3. **Do you have no access to the paper?**
   If yes → do not use this tool to download it. The tool is not designed to bypass paywalls.

## Glossary

**DOI** — Digital Object Identifier. A unique persistent identifier for a document (e.g. `10.1613/jair.49`).

**Open access** — A paper that is freely available to anyone online, typically under a Creative Commons license.

**Institutional access** — Access to a paper granted through a subscription paid by your university or research institute.

**Paywall** — A barrier that restricts access to content unless payment or authentication is provided.

**ToS (Terms of Service)** — The contractual rules set by a publisher governing how their platform can be used.
